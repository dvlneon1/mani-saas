# Brand Assets — Projeto Mani

## Logo

### Primary Logo
- **Mark:** Abstract nail curve + "M" monogram
- **Typography:** Inter Display, 700 weight, -0.5px tracking
- **Colors:** Purple #8B5CF6 (mark) + Slate #111827 (text)
- **Clear Space:** 1x mark height on all sides
- **Minimum Width:** 120px (digital), 25mm (print)

### Logo Variants
| Variant | Usage | Colors |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Primary | Light backgrounds | Mark: #8B5CF6, Text: #111827 |
| Primary Dark | Dark backgrounds | Mark: #A78BFA, Text: #F1F5F9 |
| Monochrome Light | Single color, light bg | #111827 |
| Monochrome Dark | Single color, dark bg | #FFFFFF |
| Icon Only | App icon, favicon, small spaces | #8B5CF6 / #FFFFFF |
| Reversed | On brand colors | #FFFFFF |

### Logo Don'ts
- ❌ Don't stretch or distort
- ❌ Don't change colors
- ❌ Don't add effects (shadows, gradients)
- ❌ Don't place on busy backgrounds without clear space
- ❌ Don't use mark without text unless space constrained

## Visual Tone of Voice

### Principles
1. **Clean** — Ample whitespace, clear hierarchy, no clutter
2. **Sophisticated** — Refined details, thoughtful transitions, premium feel
3. **Feminine-Professional** — Soft curves, warm accents, not stereotypical pink
4. **Photo-First** — Nail photos are hero; UI recedes

### Do's
- ✅ Generous padding (16px minimum)
- ✅ Rounded corners (12px cards, 9999px pills)
- ✅ Subtle shadows for depth
- ✅ Purple as primary action color
- ✅ Coral for secondary/accent
- ✅ High contrast text
- ✅ Smooth, springy animations

### Don'ts
- ❌ Hard edges (except dividers)
- ❌ Heavy borders
- ❌ Competing colors
- ❌ Dense information without hierarchy
- ❌ Generic stock photos
- ❌ Jarring animations
